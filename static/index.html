THE NUMBERS BEHIND THE DEMO

Fixed rates. Clear expectations.

These are the original project's teaching examples. They aren't market quotes and never update automatically.

How does this wallet work?

Every deposit is converted into GHS using a fixed example rate. A withdrawal deducts its GHS equivalent. Transactions are rounded to two GHS decimal places by Python's Decimal arithmetic. Each browser has its own wallet; sessions expire after eight hours and all data resets when the server stops. No money is transferred and no financial account is opened.
